ISTE Journal Research Publication

Volume 48 · Special Issue No. 2 · May 2025

“Smart Guide: An Interactive Driving Coach on Your Dashboard”

Journal: Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE)

Abstract:

With the rising adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) and smart cockpits, driver distraction and real-time range/safety management remain paramount. This research proposes the Smart Guide architecture—an avatar-based intelligent driving coach integrated into modern automobile dashboards.

The system combines real-time computer vision for driver attention analysis, vehicle health monitoring, predictive road risk alerts, and native Hindi language voice guidance. Laboratory tests and simulator evaluations demonstrated a significant reduction in driver cognitive strain and high alert-adherence rates.

Citation Format: Piyush Chopade et al., "Smart Guide: An Interactive Driving Coach on Your Dashboard," ISTE Journal, Vol. 48, Sp. Issue No. 2, May 2025.

AI Forest Fire Detection Architecture

Early Wildfire Identification & Intelligent Alert Dispatch

Forest fires cause catastrophic ecological degradation and wildlife fatalities every year. Traditional surveillance relies heavily on human lookout towers or delayed satellite passes which frequently detect fires only after substantial expansion.

1. Vision-Based Ingestion: Continuous frame processing from edge camera feeds and aerial drone streams.
2. Flame & Smoke Feature Extraction: Deep convolutional layers analyze color space dynamics, flickering turbulence, and rising smoke densities.
3. Low Latency Incident Dispatch: Instantaneous GPS-tagged alerting to emergency responder dashboards and forest ranger radios.

MindBridge AI System Overview

AI-Powered Early Detection System for Learning Disabilities

MindBridge AI is engineered to bridge the critical gap between early symptom emergence and formal clinical assessment for school students.

1. Multi-Disciplinary Diagnostics: Evaluates subtle reading hesitations, phonetic dysfluencies, dysgraphia handwriting jitter, and numerical sequencing hurdles.
2. Machine Learning Pattern Classifiers: Identifies underlying cognitive bottlenecks without causing stress or stigmatization to young learners.
3. Actionable Educator Dashboards: Generates personalized pedagogical recommendations tailored to each child's learning profile.
